Please read the brief overview below before we get started.

Thank you for participating. In this study, you will use a research prototype called the Learning EMR. The Learning EMR is designed to help clinicians review patient information and, in some cases, display selected data in a dedicated highlighted area. We are studying how ICU physicians interact with this type of EMR display while reviewing patient cases.

First, you will answer a few demographic questions and complete a brief questionnaire about your expectations of the system. You will then review one practice case to become familiar with the interface. After that, you will review six ICU patient cases.

For each case, please review the available information as if you were preparing to present the patient during morning rounds, then select the data elements that were most important in your reasoning about that patient. Some cases may include a dedicated highlighted area and others may not. Please use the interface naturally and rely on your own clinical judgment.

At the end of the session, you will complete the questionnaire about the Learning EMR one final time.

There are no right or wrong answers; please respond based on your own experience using the prototype.
